make.fun entry for **The Best Advice I Ever Got**: one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best advice the writer ever received.

`main.py` deterministically writes the locked line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My grandmother told me to marry the man who does the dishes without being asked, and forty years later I still test every roommate that way.
